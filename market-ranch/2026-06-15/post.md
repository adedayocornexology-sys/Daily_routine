## Hook Strategy (research-backed)

- ~2–3s to hook; aim for a 60%+ 3-second hold (5–10× more reach).
- Open on the most compelling visual (FirstBank tent), NOT a logo.
- Event recaps win with FOMO ("show 'em what they missed").
- Chosen hook: **A → B** — open FOMO, close authority/CTA.

Hook bank (swap-ready):
- A ⭐ "What you missed at the Ibom Open 👀" (FOMO)
- B "This is how you make a brand impossible to ignore." (authority)
- C "POV: your brand owns the green ⛳"
- D "What it takes to activate a brand 🎬"
- E "1 tournament. 1 brand. Total takeover."

## Production Notes
- **Music:** upbeat, confident afrobeat or corporate-energy track (check IG's trending audio for reach).

- **Transitions:** crossfade or a clean whip-pan between clips.
- **Text style:** bold sans-serif, white with subtle shadow, lower-third or centered.
- **First 1s = hook:** lead with the FirstBank tent or the wide course shot to stop the scroll.
